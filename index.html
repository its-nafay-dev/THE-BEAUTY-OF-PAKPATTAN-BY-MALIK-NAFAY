SPIRITUAL CAPITAL OF PUNJAB

The Spiritual & Cultural Legacy

Pakpattan, historically known as Ajodhan, is an ancient city located on the high bank of the old Sutlej River. It earned its title "Pakpattan" meaning "Pure Ferry" after the revered Sufi saint Hazrat Baba Fariduddin Ganjshakar (R.A) settled here.

Sufi Sanctuary

Pakpattan stands as the global epicenter of Chisti Sufi philosophy. People from across continents gather to embrace messages of universal love, peace, and spiritual devotion.

Colonial & Ancient Gates

The city preserves ancient walled heritage featuring iconic gateways such as the Farid Gate, reflecting traditional trade routes and timeless Punjabi masonry.

Sutlej River Ecosystem

Surrounded by fertile agricultural plains, scenic canals, and lush gardens, Pakpattan combines sacred history with peaceful rural landscape aesthetics.

EXPLORE PAKPATTAN

12 Featured Landmarks & Attractions

Spiritual Site

1. Darbar Hazrat Baba Farid (R.A)

The sacred heart of Pakpattan. The shrine of 12th-century Sufi saint Baba Farid Ganj Shakar attracts millions annually for spiritual solace, communal meals (Langar), and timeless Qawwali melodies.

Open 24/7 Central Old City
Public Recreation

2. Sair Gah Park

A family-friendly green oasis equipped with manicured lawns, colorful flowerbeds, jogging tracks, and children's play zones, serving as Pakpattan's premier evening relaxation destination.

06:00 AM - 10:00 PM Stadium Road
Administrative Heritage

3. Town Hall Pakpattan

An iconic municipal edifice exhibiting classic colonial architectural motifs. It stands as a civic testament to Pakpattan's historical urban evolution and administrative legacy.

09:00 AM - 05:00 PM Civil Lines
Religious Monument

4. Masjid Waris Shah

A peaceful Islamic sanctuary associated with Punjabi literary heritage and spiritual reflections. The mosque displays traditional tilework and serene prayer courtyards.

Open all prayer times Old City District
Waterfront Recreation

5. Canal Park

Situated alongside Pakpattan Canal, this scenic park offers cool breezes, shaded seating, and waterfront promenade walks preferred by local families and nature enthusiasts.

Sunrise - Sunset Canal Bank Road
Historical Shrine

6. Panipat Darbar Sharif

A respected Sufi pilgrimage site reflecting deep historical links with regional Sufi lineages. Known for intricate wood craftsmanship and tranquil devotional ambience.

05:00 AM - 10:00 PM Eastern Suburbs
Transport Heritage

7. Pakpattan Railway Station

Built during the British Raj era, this station features classic red-brick arches, vintage signaling equipment, and a historical atmosphere connecting Pakpattan across Punjab.

24 Hours Operation Station Road
Sufi Shrine

8. Darbar Hazrat Khawaja Aziz Makki

A revered shrine dedicated to Khawaja Aziz Makki (R.A). Pilgrims visit for spiritual meditation, blessings, and quiet reflection amidst classic Islamic archways.

06:00 AM - 09:00 PM Aziz Makki Road
Commercial Market

9. Dargah Bazar

The historic shopping heart of the city. Flanked by traditional shops selling Sufi handicrafts, traditional sweets (Sohan Halwa), attar perfumes, and vibrant Punjabi textiles.

09:00 AM - 11:00 PM Shrine Perimeter
Memorial Park

10. Baba Farid Park

A spacious municipal park named in memory of the great Sufi poet. Offers lush green lawns, walking paths, shade trees, and evening community gathering spots.

05:00 AM - 09:00 PM Bypass Road
Modern Landmark

11. Jamal Chok Multi-Color Tower

A modern architectural square centerpiece. Illumined with multi-colored LED displays at night, it serves as a major landmark icon and city navigation junction.

Best Viewed at Night Jamal Chowk Roundabout
Industrial / Ecological

12. Kumhariwala Hydro Power Project

An impressive green energy initiative along the canal system. The surrounding water engineering structures and open landscapes make it a popular technical and scenic visiting point.

Daytime Access Canal Headworks
