SPIRITUAL CAPITAL

The Legacy of Pakpattan

Hazrat Baba Farid (R.A)

Pakpattan is renowned worldwide for the shrine of the great Sufi poet Hazrat Baba Fariduddin Ganjshakar (R.A), attracting millions seeking peace and spiritual solace.

Historic Gates

The city's historic entrances, including the famous Farid Gate, showcase classic architecture connecting ancient trade routes with modern culture.

Natural Beauty

Flanked near the Sutlej River basin, the surrounding lush green fields and agricultural beauty highlight the natural charm of Punjab.

Famous Landmarks

Spiritual

Darbar Hazrat Baba Farid

The central spiritual hub of Pakpattan, world-famous for Sufi music, Qawwali, and historic gatherings.

Historical

Farid Gate Pakpattan

One of the oldest surviving historical city gates, serving as a bustling gateway to the traditional markets.

Nature

Sutlej River Plains

Scenic green landscapes and fertile plains along the Sutlej River belt surrounding Pakpattan District.
